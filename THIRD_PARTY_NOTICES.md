## Rules

- CC0 and MIT sources are preferred for prototypes, but still require a record.
- GPL or unclear copyleft code requires an explicit legal/distribution decision.
- Store-extracted, ripped, or unlicensed copyrighted assets are prohibited.
- Generated/vendor files are not hand-edited and are reported separately from authored code.
