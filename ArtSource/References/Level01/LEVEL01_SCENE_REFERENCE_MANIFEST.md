## Production rules

- Preserve the exact established appearance of Hayreddin Barbarossa and every recurring
  character.
- Reuse registered Level 01 models. If a visible object has no registered authored model,
  stop and create/approve its isolated single-view reference before placing it visibly.
- Do not regenerate or reconvert an asset that already has a valid prepared model.
- Use the fictional lion-wave banner only; do not introduce real national flags.
- Scene references and automated captures are preparation evidence, not user art approval.
  A revision remains unapproved until the user reviews it in Unity and explicitly approves it.
